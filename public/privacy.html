Privacy Policy

Effective October 21, 2026

Pricr is a quoting app for landscaping, hardscape and snow-removal businesses. This policy explains what information we collect when you use the Pricr app and website, how we use it, and the choices you have. If you have a question, email info@pricr.com.

Information we collect

Account information. Your email address and a password when you sign up. Passwords are stored by our authentication provider in hashed form; we never see them.

Business information you enter. Your business name, address, phone, email, logo and brand color; the services, materials, equipment and rates you charge; target margins, tax rate and quote terms; and the names and pay rates of employees you add to price labor.

Quotes and your customers' details. The quotes you build, including the customer name, phone number and property address you enter, measurements, notes, scheduled dates, and any site photos you attach.

Team information. If you invite teammates, their email addresses and roles, and who created, approved or sent each quote.

Agreements. When and which version of our market insights agreement you accepted.

Technical information. Basic information needed to run and secure the service, such as device type, app version, and server logs (for example, IP address and time of a request).

We do not use advertising trackers, and we do not track you across other companies' apps or websites.

How we use information

  • To run Pricr: sign you in, save your settings, price quotes, create PDFs, and let your team work together.
  • To send quotes and notifications you ask us to send.
  • To create AI concept previews when you use that feature (see below).
  • To provide support, fix problems, keep the service secure and prevent abuse.
  • To improve Pricr, using information about how features are used.
  • To produce anonymous market insights, as described next.

Market insights

When you create an account you agree that Pricr may combine pricing data from many businesses into anonymous market reports and a weekly newsletter (for example, average prices, typical margins and snow pricing trends in an area).

  • We use: the rates and margins you set, which services you quote, quote totals, general area (such as town or ZIP code) and dates.
  • We never use or share: customer names, phone numbers, emails or addresses, your photos, or your company name.
  • Numbers are combined across several businesses and shown as averages and ranges, so no single company can be identified.

To stop contributing, delete your account in Settings.

AI concept previews

If you choose to generate a concept preview of finished work, the site photo you select and a description of the work on the quote are sent to our AI image provider (OpenAI) to create the image. This happens only when you use the feature. The provider processes this data under its API terms to return the image to you.

Who we share information with

We do not sell your information. We share it only:

  • With your team. People you invite to your business can see its settings and quotes according to their role.
  • With service providers who run parts of Pricr for us: Supabase (database, sign-in and file storage), Vercel (website hosting), EmailJS (sending quote emails) and OpenAI (AI previews). They may only use the data to provide their service to us.
  • With the people you send quotes to, when you choose to send or share a quote.
  • When required by law, or to protect the rights, safety and security of our users, the public or Pricr.
  • In a business transfer, such as a merger or acquisition, in which case this policy continues to apply to your information.

Your customers' information

When you enter a customer's details into Pricr, you are responsible for having the right to do so and for how you use those details with your customer. We store and process them only to provide the service to you.

Keeping and deleting information

We keep your information while your account is active. You can delete your account at any time in the app under Settings → General settings → Delete my account. Deleting an owner account permanently deletes the business, its services, rates, quotes and team access. Deleting a teammate account removes that person's login; quotes they made stay with the business. Deleted data may remain in encrypted backups for a limited time before it is overwritten. Anonymous, combined market statistics that no longer identify you are not removed.

Security

We use industry-standard safeguards, including encrypted connections (HTTPS) and access controls that keep each business's data separate. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Your choices and rights

You can view and update your business information in the app, and delete your account in Settings. Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or get a copy of your personal information. To make a request, email info@pricr.com. We will not discriminate against you for exercising these rights.

Children

Pricr is a business tool and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as Pricr changes. We will change the date at the top, and if the changes are significant we will let you know in the app or by email.

Contact

Questions or requests: info@pricr.com
