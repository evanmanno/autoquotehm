Common questions

How do I add my services and prices?
Go to Settings → Business settings. Set your services and rates under Service pricing, your materials and equipment, and your labor rate, margin and employees under Rates, margins & employees. Every new quote prices itself from these.

How do I invite my team?
Go to Settings → Team & invites, enter their email and role, and send them the invite. They sign up with that exact email address and join your business automatically.

How do I set up snow pricing?
In Settings → Service pricing, set your snow service rates. At the bottom, choose how snowfall depth and building class change those rates, either as a percent or as your own dollar amounts.

Can my crew use Pricr in Spanish or Portuguese?
Yes. Go to Settings → General settings → Language. Quotes your customers receive stay in English.

I forgot my password.
Email us from the address you sign in with and we'll help you reset it. If you're signed in, you can change your password in Settings → General settings → Account.

How do I delete my account?
Go to Settings → General settings → Delete my account. Deleting an owner account permanently removes the business and its data. See our Privacy Policy for details.
